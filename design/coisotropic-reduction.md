# Coisotropic reduction

This is the detailed design of part 40 of `DESIGN.md`, *Coisotropic
reduction*. It covers the milestones *Regular coisotropic reduction* and
*Clean reduction of Lagrangian submanifolds* of
`atlas/symplectic-group-actions.md`, which follow McDuff–Salamon
[McDuffSalamon2017], §5.4 (Lemma 5.4.1, condition (R), Propositions 5.4.5
and 5.4.7). For leaves, slices and the rank theorem it follows Lee
(J. M. Lee, *Introduction to Smooth Manifolds*, 2nd ed., Springer GTM 218,
2013, Theorem 4.12, Theorem 4.31 and Chapter 19).

Paths of new files are under `SymplecticGeometry/`; Tau Ceti paths are
relative to `.lake/packages/TauCeti/TauCeti/`, as in `DESIGN.md`. Manifolds
follow *Manifolds, families and signs* of `DESIGN.md`: real, modelled on
finite-dimensional spaces, smooth (`∞`), and boundaryless where the
Frobenius theorem is used. As in the merged
`Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/CharacteristicFoliation.lean`, declarations
about a Tau Ceti notion go in its namespace: `TauCeti.Foliation` for
foliations, `TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.IsNondegenerate` for coisotropic and
Lagrangian maps. Below, `f : N → M` is the coisotropic submanifold $Q$, over
`J : ModelWithCorners ℝ F H'`, in a manifold `M` over
`I : ModelWithCorners ℝ E H` with a closed nondegenerate
`σ : TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm I M`; its codimension is
`finrank ℝ E - finrank ℝ F`.

## What exists, and what the part adds

- **Foliations and leaves.** Tau Ceti's `TauCeti.Foliation I n M k` is an
  involutive distribution of rank `k` (`Geometry/Manifold/Foliation/Basic.lean`).
  Its leaves are classes of `TauCeti.Foliation.SameLeaf`, the equivalence
  relation generated by curves tangent to the distribution, with
  `leaf`, `sameLeaf_equivalence`, `SameLeaf.induction_on`,
  `sameLeaf_of_contMDiffOn_of_tangent` and `isPathConnected_leaf`
  (`Foliation/Leaf.lean`). The foliation by translates of a subspace is
  `Foliation.ofSubmodule`, with leaves `leaf_ofSubmodule`.
- **Characteristic foliations.** `TauCeti.SmoothForm.characteristicFoliation`
  (`Geometry/Manifold/TwoForm/Kernel.lean`), and for a coisotropic
  immersion `IsCoisotropicMap.characteristicFoliation`, of rank
  `finrank ℝ E - finrank ℝ F`, with `characteristicFoliation_distribution`
  and `map_characteristicFoliation_distribution`
  (`Geometry/Symplectic/Lagrangian/Presymplectic.lean`). Involutivity is
  `TauCeti.SmoothForm.isInvolutiveDistribution_ker_bilinFormAt`.
- **Flat charts.** Tau Ceti's `TauCeti.IsInvolutiveDistribution.exists_flatChart`
  (`Geometry/Manifold/Distribution/FlatChart.lean`), and the library's
  `exists_flatPartialDiffeomorph`, a flat chart into a product whose target
  contains the unit ball (`Geometry/Manifold/Distribution/FlatChart.lean`).
- **Descent.** The library's `TauCeti.SmoothForm.IsCharacteristicSubmersion`
  and `IsCharacteristicSubmersion.existsUnique_isSymplectic_mpullback_eq`
  (`Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/CharacteristicFoliation.lean`): a closed
  form is the pullback of a unique symplectic form along a surjective
  submersion with preconnected fibres whose vertical spaces are its
  kernels. This is the form of Tau Ceti's
  `TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.existsUnique_isSymplectic_mpullback_eq` that the
  part uses, as the approving review of #2250 asks.
- **Gluing charts.** `TauCeti.chartedSpaceOfIsOpenEmbedding` and
  `isManifold_chartedSpaceOfIsOpenEmbedding`
  (`Geometry/Manifold/Gluing/Basic.lean`): a space covered by open
  embeddings of manifolds, with smooth overlap maps, is a manifold.
- **Submersions.** `Manifold.IsSubmersion.contMDiff_comp_iff`,
  `existsUnique_contMDiff_comp_eq` and `isOpenMap`
  (`Geometry/Manifold/Submersion/Basic.lean`); uniqueness of a quotient
  smooth structure, `ChartedSpace.exists_diffeomorph_apply_eq_self_of_isSubmersion`
  (`Submersion/Diffeomorph.lean`); immersions through a submersion,
  `IsSubmersion.isImmersion_iff_ker_mfderiv_comp` (`Submersion/Descent.lean`).
- **Rank theorem.** `ContMDiffOn.exists_openPartialHomeomorph_comp_eq_mfderiv_comp`
  (`Geometry/Manifold/ConstantRank.lean`), for maps into a boundaryless
  manifold; involutivity of the kernels of any smooth map,
  `ContMDiff.isInvolutiveDistribution_ker_mfderiv`
  (`Geometry/Manifold/Distribution/Kernel.lean`); local injectivity of
  immersions, `ContMDiffAt.exists_mem_nhds_injOn`
  (`Geometry/Manifold/MFDeriv/LocalInjectivity.lean`).
- **Disjoint unions.** `ChartedSpace.sigma` and `IsManifold.sigma`
  (`Geometry/Manifold/Instances/Sigma.lean`).
- **Lagrangian maps.** `IsLagrangianMap`, `IsLagrangianImmersion`,
  `IsLagrangianEmbedding`; `isLagrangianMap_comp_submersion_iff` and
  `IsLagrangianMap.isLagrangianImmersion_of_comp_submersion`
  (`Geometry/Symplectic/Lagrangian/Submersion.lean`); linear reduction
  `TauCeti.SymplecticForm.IsLagrangian.reduction_map` and
  `IsCoisotropic.finrank_reducedSpace_add_finrank`
  (`Geometry/Symplectic/LinearReduction.lean`), with the library's

Neither library has a leaf space, condition (R), transport along leaves,
clean intersections, or either milestone. The part adds the smooth leaf

characteristic one, then the two milestones on top of it.

## Decisions

`DESIGN.md` leaves three questions to this file: how $Q$ is given, the
leaf space and its charts, and clean intersections. The other paragraphs
settle what the milestones need beyond them.

**How $Q$ is given.** By a map `f : N → M` with
`hσ.IsCoisotropicMap J f`, `Manifold.IsImmersion J I ∞ f` and
`ContMDiff J I ∞ f`, the hypotheses of `IsCoisotropicMap.characteristicFoliation`.
The reduction theorem and the Lagrangian statements use only the
presymplectic form `f^* σ` on `N`, and state their hypotheses on `N` and
on the parametrization `C` of the intersection, so they are stated for
coisotropic immersions. An embedded $Q$, Tau Ceti's
`IsCoisotropicEmbedding`, gives these hypotheses through its fields
`isImmersion` and `isCoisotropicMap`; the milestones are the more general
statements, as `AGENTS.md` allows. The topology that `M` induces on the
image matters in one place: the plan's connectedness hypothesis of the
injective case is about subsets of `M`. For an embedded $Q$ a corollary
turns it into the hypothesis on `C` (see `Lagrangian/Injective.lean`).

**The leaf space and its charts.** The leaf space of a foliation `𝓕` of `N`
is the quotient `TauCeti.Foliation.LeafSpace 𝓕` of `N` by the setoid of
`SameLeaf`, with the quotient topology. Condition (R) is stated with
parametrized slices: a slice is a smooth map `s : U → N` from an open set
`U` of the model space `EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin (finrank ℝ F - k))`, `k` the
rank, whose derivative at each point has range complementary to the
distribution, and which meets each leaf at most once. The foliation is
regular when every point of `N` is in the range of a slice. This loses
nothing against the book's submanifolds $S$: a submanifold through $p$ with
the complement condition has dimension `finrank ℝ F - k`, and a chart of it
near $p$ is such a slice, since a smaller part of $S$ still meets each leaf
at most once. The charts of the leaf space are the inverses of
`leafSpaceMk ∘ s`, which are open embeddings; the overlap maps are smooth
because two slices through one leaf are related by transport along the
leaf, which is smooth (`Foliation/Transport.lean`). Tau Ceti's
`chartedSpaceOfIsOpenEmbedding` then glues them. The model is the fixed
space `EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin (finrank ℝ F - k))`, as Tau Ceti's quotient
manifold theorem uses `ProperSMul.QuotientModelSpace`, and the charted
space is a definition marked `@[instance_reducible]`, not an instance,
since it depends on the proof of regularity.

Hausdorffness of the leaf space is not used by the construction, the
submersion, or the reduced form. The theorem for *Regular coisotropic
reduction* therefore drops the plan's hypothesis that the leaf space is
Hausdorff; with it, `T2Space` holds by assumption and the leaf space is a
manifold in the book's sense. The injective case of *Clean reduction of
Lagrangian submanifolds* does need it, and takes `T2Space` of the leaf
space of $Q$ as a hypothesis, which the plan's regularity includes.
Without it the image of $L$ can be a line with a doubled point: in
$\mathbb R^4$ with $\omega=dx\wedge d\xi+dy\wedge d\eta$, take
$Q=\{\xi=0\}\setminus\{x=y=0\}$ and $L=\{\xi=\eta=0\}$. No injective
immersion of a Hausdorff 1-manifold has that image. The general and
transverse cases do not use it.
Second countability passes from `N` to the leaf space, since the quotient
map is open.

**Clean intersection for maps.** The Lagrangian submanifold is
`g : P → M`, over `K : ModelWithCorners ℝ G H''`. Its intersection with $Q$
is given by a smooth embedding `c : C → N` whose range lies in
`f ⁻¹' range g` and is open and closed in it, for the topology of `N`,
such that whenever `g y = f (c x)` the range of `mfderiv (f ∘ c) x` is the
intersection of the ranges of `mfderiv f (c x)` and `mfderiv g y`. This is
the predicate `SymplecticGeometry.IsCleanIntersection`. Following *Submanifolds* of
`DESIGN.md`, `C` has one model, so it has one dimension. Since `range c`
need only be a union of components of the intersection, the theorem
applies to the part of each dimension of an intersection whose components
have different dimensions. The plan's case of one dimension is
`range c = f ⁻¹' range g`, where `range (f ∘ c) = range f ∩ range g`.
Transversality is the hypothesis that those two ranges span the tangent
space of `M`; it gets no predicate of its own.

**The Lagrangian immersion.** On `C`, the map `leafSpaceMk ∘ c` into the
leaf space $\bar Q$ has constant rank $n-k$, and the kernel of its
derivative is the clean-intersection distribution
$T_pL\cap(T_pQ)^\omega$. The general case does not build a leaf space of
`C`: Tau Ceti's rank theorem factors `leafSpaceMk ∘ c`, near each point, as
a submersion onto an open set of `EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin (finrank ℝ G - k))`
followed by an immersion, which is Lagrangian by
`isLagrangianMap_comp_submersion_iff`. Countably many of these charts cover
`C` when `C` is second countable, and the source is their disjoint union
`Σ i : ℕ, U i` (`ChartedSpace.sigma`), which is Hausdorff and second
countable; it is the countable union of charts that the page describes.
In the injective case, the kernels form a foliation of `C`
(`Foliation.ofConstantRank`), which is regular by the slices on which
`leafSpaceMk ∘ c` is injective, and its leaf space maps injectively to
$\bar Q$ when every fibre of `leafSpaceMk ∘ c` is preconnected; the
induced map is then the injective Lagrangian immersion. When the leaf
space of $Q$ is Hausdorff, so is this source, since the map is injective
and continuous. The reduced form is passed as a hypothesis
`τ : SmoothTwoForm 𝓘(ℝ, _) (LeafSpace _)` with `τ.IsSymplectic` and
`mpullback leafSpaceMk τ = mpullback f σ`, which by the first milestone
determines it; no definition of the reduced form is made.

**Why a folder `Coisotropic/`.** The merged `Neighborhood/Coisotropic.lean`
is the coisotropic neighbourhood theorem, about a neighbourhood of $Q$ in
$M$, and `Manifold/CharacteristicFoliation.lean` gives local quotients on
small open sets of $Q$. Reduction is about the global leaf space of $Q$ and
about Lagrangian submanifolds meeting $Q$, uses neither file's results
beyond the descent theorem, and has its own milestones, so it gets its own
folder, as the approving review of #2250 asks this file to say.

## Files and declarations

### `Geometry/Manifold/Foliation/Transport.lean`

Transport along the leaves of a foliation `𝓕 : TauCeti.Foliation I ∞ M k`
of a boundaryless manifold.

- `TauCeti.Foliation.exists_foliatedChart`: around every point `x`, an open
  `U ∋ x` and a diffeomorphism of `U` onto the product of the unit balls of
  `F₁` and `F₂`, finite-dimensional with `finrank ℝ F₁ = k` and
  `finrank ℝ E = k + finrank ℝ F₂` (such as `EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin k)` and
  `EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin (finrank ℝ E - k))`),
  sending `x` to `0`, whose derivative carries the distribution onto the
  first factor, and such that two points of `U` with the same second
  coordinate lie on the same leaf. From `exists_flatPartialDiffeomorph`,
  restricted to the preimage of the product of balls, as in the merged
  `TauCeti.SmoothForm.exists_isCharacteristicSubmersion`; the segment
  between two points of a plaque is a curve tangent to `𝓕`, so
  `sameLeaf_of_contMDiffOn_of_tangent` applies.
- `TauCeti.Foliation.SameLeaf.exists_transport`: if `𝓕.SameLeaf x y`,
  there are a neighbourhood `V` of `x` and a map `φ : M → M`, smooth on
  `V`, with `φ x = y` and `𝓕.SameLeaf z (φ z)` for `z ∈ V`. Within one
  plaque chart, `φ` keeps the second coordinate and moves the first; for a
  tangent curve, cover it by plaque charts (a Lebesgue number on `[0, 1]`)
  and compose; for the relation, `SameLeaf.induction_on`.
- `TauCeti.Foliation.isOpen_saturation`: the union of the leaves through an
  open set is open, by `exists_transport` from `y` back to a point of the
  set.

### `Geometry/Manifold/Foliation/LeafSpace/Basic.lean`

The leaf space as a topological space, and slices.

- `TauCeti.Foliation.leafSetoid 𝓕 : Setoid M` (the relation `SameLeaf`),
  `TauCeti.Foliation.LeafSpace 𝓕 := Quotient 𝓕.leafSetoid`, and
  `TauCeti.Foliation.leafSpaceMk 𝓕 : M → 𝓕.LeafSpace`.
- API: `leafSpaceMk_eq_leafSpaceMk_iff` (equal exactly on one leaf),
  `preimage_leafSpaceMk_singleton` (the fibre is `𝓕.leaf x`),
  `leafSpaceMk_surjective`, `continuous_leafSpaceMk`,
  `isOpenMap_leafSpaceMk` (from `isOpen_saturation`),
  `isOpenQuotientMap_leafSpaceMk`, and the instance
  `SecondCountableTopology 𝓕.LeafSpace` for second countable `M`.
- `TauCeti.Foliation.IsSlice 𝓕 (s : U → M)`, for
  `U : Opens (EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin (finrank ℝ E - k)))`: a structure with
  `contMDiff`, `isCompl_range_mfderiv` (the range of `mfderiv s u` and
  `𝓕.distribution (s u)` are complements) and `injective_of_sameLeaf`.
  `TauCeti.Foliation.IsRegular 𝓕`: every point is in the range of a slice.
- `IsSlice.exists_retraction`: near `s u`, a map `r` into `U`, smooth there,
  with `r (s v) = v` near `u`, `𝓕.SameLeaf z (s (r z))`, and
  `mfderiv r (s u)` zero on `𝓕.distribution (s u)`. In a plaque
  chart at `s u`, the second coordinate composed with `s` is a local
  diffeomorphism at `u`, by the complement condition and the inverse
  function theorem; `r` is its local inverse after the second coordinate.
- `IsSlice.isOpenEmbedding_leafSpaceMk_comp`: `leafSpaceMk ∘ s` is an open
  embedding (injective by `injective_of_sameLeaf`, open by
  `exists_retraction` and `isOpenMap_leafSpaceMk`).

### `Geometry/Manifold/Foliation/LeafSpace/Smooth.lean`

The smooth structure of the leaf space of a regular foliation.

- `IsSlice.exists_contMDiffAt_transition`: for slices `s₁`, `s₂` with
  `leafSpaceMk (s₁ u) = leafSpaceMk (s₂ v)`, a map `T`, smooth at `u`, with
  `leafSpaceMk ∘ s₂ ∘ T` eventually equal to `leafSpaceMk ∘ s₁`; `T` is the
  retraction onto `s₂` after the transport from `s₁ u` to `s₂ v`. This is
  the hypothesis of Tau Ceti's gluing.
- `TauCeti.Foliation.leafSpaceChartedSpace (h : 𝓕.IsRegular)`: the charted
  space on `𝓕.LeafSpace` modelled on
  `EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin (finrank ℝ E - k))`, from
  `chartedSpaceOfIsOpenEmbedding`; `@[instance_reducible]`.
- `isManifold_leafSpace`, `isSubmersion_leafSpaceMk` and
  `ker_mfderiv_leafSpaceMk` (the kernel of the derivative of `leafSpaceMk`
  at `x` is `𝓕.distribution x`), each under `letI := leafSpaceChartedSpace h`.
- An example: `Foliation.ofSubmodule S` is regular, with a complement of
  `S` as slice, and its leaf space is Hausdorff.

### `Geometry/Symplectic/Coisotropic/Reduction.lean`

Regular coisotropic reduction. For a coisotropic immersion `f` with
`hR : (h.characteristicFoliation hi hf hσc hn).IsRegular` (with
`hn : 1 ≤ ∞`), and the
charted space `leafSpaceChartedSpace hR`:

- `IsCoisotropicMap.isCharacteristicSubmersion_leafSpaceMk`: `leafSpaceMk`
  is a characteristic submersion of `f^* σ`. Its fibres are leaves, which
  are path-connected (`isPathConnected_leaf`), and its kernels are those of
  the form (`ker_mfderiv_leafSpaceMk`,
  `characteristicFoliation_distribution`).
- `IsCoisotropicMap.isSubmersion_leafSpaceMk_and_existsUnique_isSymplectic_mpullback_eq`,
  the milestone, with `n k : ℕ`, `finrank ℝ E = 2 * n` and
  `finrank ℝ F + k = finrank ℝ E`: the kernels of `f^* σ` form an
  involutive distribution; the leaf space is a manifold and `leafSpaceMk` a
  submersion; any other charted space on the leaf space, for any topology,
  for which `leafSpaceMk` is a submersion is diffeomorphic to it by the
  identity (`ChartedSpace.exists_diffeomorph_apply_eq_self_of_isSubmersion`);
  the model space has dimension `2 * n - 2 * k`; and there is a unique
  symplectic `τ` on the leaf space with `mpullback leafSpaceMk τ =
  mpullback f σ` (`IsCharacteristicSubmersion.existsUnique_isSymplectic_mpullback_eq`).
  Milestone: *Regular coisotropic reduction*.

### `Geometry/Manifold/Distribution/ConstantRank.lean`

- `ContMDiff.isContMDiffDistribution_ker_mfderiv`: the kernels of the
  derivative of a smooth map of constant rank `r` from a boundaryless
  manifold to a boundaryless manifold form a smooth distribution of rank
  `finrank ℝ E - r`, spanned in the coordinates of Tau Ceti's rank theorem
  by constant vector fields.
- `TauCeti.Foliation.ofConstantRank`: the foliation of these kernels, with
  involutivity from `ContMDiff.isInvolutiveDistribution_ker_mfderiv`, its
  `ofConstantRank_distribution` (`@[simp]`), and
  `SameLeaf.apply_eq_of_ofConstantRank`: the map is constant on each leaf.
  The fibres of a submersion are an example.

### `Geometry/Manifold/CleanIntersection.lean`

- `SymplecticGeometry.IsCleanIntersection I J K L f g c`, `L` the model of `C`:
  the structure of *Decisions*, with
  fields `isSmoothEmbedding`, `range_subset` (`range c ⊆ f ⁻¹' range g`),
  `isClopen_range` (the preimage of `range c` in the subtype
  `f ⁻¹' range g` is open and closed) and `range_mfderiv_comp`.
- An example: a smooth embedding meets itself cleanly, with `c = id`.

### `LinearAlgebra/Symplectic/Subspace.lean` (merged file)

  Lagrangian `Λ` and any subspace `W` of `V`,
  `2 * finrank (Λ ⊓ W) + finrank V = 2 * finrank (Λ ⊓ ω.orthogonal W) + 2 * finrank W`.

### `Geometry/Symplectic/Coisotropic/Lagrangian/Basic.lean`

The pointwise statements. With `f`, `hR` and `τ` as above (of `τ`, only
`τ.IsNondegenerate` is used), `g` with
`hσ.IsLagrangianMap K g` and `IsImmersion K I ∞ g`, and
`hc : IsCleanIntersection I J K L f g c`:

- `Foliation.ker_mfderiv_leafSpaceMk_comp`, in
  `Foliation/LeafSpace/Smooth.lean`: the kernel of the
  derivative of `leafSpaceMk ∘ c` at `x` is the preimage under
  `mfderiv c x` of `(h.characteristicFoliation …).distribution (c x)`; its
  image under `mfderiv (f ∘ c) x` is $T_pL\cap(T_pQ)^\omega$
  (`IsCleanIntersection.map_ker_mfderiv_leafSpaceMk_comp`).
- `IsCleanIntersection.isLagrangianMap_leafSpaceMk_comp`: the range of the
  derivative of `leafSpaceMk ∘ c` is Lagrangian for `τ`: it is the image of
  $T_pL\cap T_pQ$ in the linear reduction of $T_pQ$, which is Lagrangian by
  `SymplecticForm.IsLagrangian.reduction_map`, under the symplectomorphism
  `isSymplectomorphism_reducedSpaceEquivTangentSpace`.
- `IsCleanIntersection.mfderivRank_leafSpaceMk_comp_eq`: the rank is
  `finrank ℝ G - (finrank ℝ E - finrank ℝ F)` everywhere.
- `IsCleanIntersection.isLagrangianImmersion_leafSpaceMk_comp_of_transverse`:
  if the tangent spaces of `f` and `g` span at every common point, then
  $T_pL\cap(T_pQ)^\omega=(T_pL+T_pQ)^\omega=0$, so `leafSpaceMk ∘ c` is a
  Lagrangian immersion, for nonempty `C` with `L.Boundaryless`.

### `Geometry/Symplectic/Coisotropic/Lagrangian/Immersion.lean`

- `IsCleanIntersection.exists_isLagrangianImmersion_range_eq_image`: for
  second countable `N` (then `C` is too, `c` being an embedding) and
  nonempty boundaryless `C`, there are a type `S` with a charted space on
  `EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin (finrank ℝ G - (finrank ℝ E - finrank ℝ F)))`,
  a smooth manifold, Hausdorff and second countable, and a Lagrangian
  immersion `ι : S → LeafSpace` for `τ` with
  `range ι = leafSpaceMk '' range c`. The source is `Σ i : ℕ, U i` as in
  *Decisions*, by the rank theorem and `mfderivRank_leafSpaceMk_comp_eq`.

### `Geometry/Symplectic/Coisotropic/Lagrangian/Injective.lean`

- `IsCleanIntersection.isRegular_ofConstantRank`: the foliation
  `Foliation.ofConstantRank (leafSpaceMk ∘ c)` of `C` is regular. Its
  slices are small transversals on which `leafSpaceMk ∘ c` is injective
  (`ContMDiffAt.exists_mem_nhds_injOn` applied to the restriction, an
  immersion); two points of one slice on one leaf have the same image.
- `IsCleanIntersection.injective_leafSpaceLift`: if
  `(leafSpaceMk ∘ c) ⁻¹' {b}` is preconnected for every `b`, the map from
  the leaf space of `C` to the leaf space of `N` induced by
  `leafSpaceMk ∘ c` is injective. Each leaf of `C` in such a fibre is open
  in it, by the rank theorem, so the fibre is a single leaf.
- `IsCleanIntersection.isPreconnected_preimage_leafSpaceMk_comp`: for an
  embedded $Q$ (`hσ.IsCoisotropicEmbedding J f`), if
  `range g ∩ f '' (leafSpaceMk ⁻¹' {b})` is preconnected, so is
  `(leafSpaceMk ∘ c) ⁻¹' {b}`. The embedding `f ∘ c` maps this fibre
  homeomorphically onto the part of that set in `f '' range c`, which is
  open and closed in it by `isClopen_range`.
- `IsCleanIntersection.exists_isLagrangianImmersion_range_eq_image_and`,
  the milestone, for a Lagrangian embedding `g` and second countable `N`:
  the general statement of `Immersion.lean`; under transversality,
  `isLagrangianImmersion_leafSpaceMk_comp_of_transverse`; and, if the leaf
  space of $Q$ is Hausdorff (`T2Space`) and every fibre of
  `leafSpaceMk ∘ c` is preconnected, a Hausdorff second countable manifold
  with an injective Lagrangian immersion onto `leafSpaceMk '' range c`, the leaf
  space of `C` with the induced map (smooth by
  `IsSubmersion.contMDiff_comp_iff`, an immersion by
  `isImmersion_iff_ker_mfderiv_comp`, Lagrangian by
  `isLagrangianMap_comp_submersion_iff`). No embedding is claimed.
  Milestone: *Clean reduction of Lagrangian submanifolds*.
- `IsCleanIntersection.exists_injective_isLagrangianImmersion_of_isCoisotropicEmbedding`:
  the injective case with the plan's hypothesis, for an embedded $Q$ with
  Hausdorff leaf space when `range g ∩ f '' (leafSpaceMk ⁻¹' {b})` is
  preconnected for every `b`, by `isPreconnected_preimage_leafSpaceMk_comp`.
